The Haandi Experience
The haandi is sealed with atta dough pressed firmly around the rim. Steam cycles inside for 25–90 minutes, driving masala flavours into every fibre. The seal is broken at your table. That moment — the cloud of aromatic steam — is why you came to Dum & Desi.
⏱ Please allow 45–90 minutes · Sealed & opened at your table
